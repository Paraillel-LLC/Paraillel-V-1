

Grade Level: 3-5
Subject: Math
Topic: Numbers

Assignment: "Number Sense Challenge"

Objective:
Students will demonstrate their understanding of numbers and their ability to apply mathematical concepts through a series of multiple choice questions.

Structure:
The assignment will consist of 5 multiple choice questions, each with 4 options. Students will choose the correct answer from the given options. The questions will progressively increase in difficulty.

Instructions:
1. Read each question carefully and choose the best answer from the given options.
2. Circle your answer on the answer sheet provided.
3. You have 30 minutes to complete the assignment.
4. Make sure to show all your work and use any necessary mathematical strategies.

Questions:

1. What is the value of the underlined digit in the number 643?
a. 4
b. 400
c. 40
d. 4000

2. Which of the following is an odd number?
a. 24
b. 38
c. 57
d. 72

3. What is the product of 6 and 9?
a. 54
b. 15
c. 60
d. 65

4. Which of the following fractions is equivalent to 3/5?
a. 9/10
b. 6/8
c. 5/9
d. 4/6

5. What is the place value of the underlined digit in the number 35,724?
a. thousands
b. tens
c. ones
d. hundreds

Alignment:
This assignment aligns with the following 4th grade Common Core State Standards for Mathematics:

- 4.NBT.A.1: Recognize that in a multi-digit whole number, a digit in one place represents ten times what it represents in the place to its right.
- 4.NBT.A.2: Read and write multi-digit whole numbers using base-ten numerals, number names, and expanded form.
- 4.NBT.B.3: Use place value understanding to round multi-digit whole numbers to any place.
- 4.NBT.B.4: Compare two multi-digit numbers based on meanings of the digits in each place, using >, =, and < symbols.

Grading:
Each question will be worth 4 points, for a total of 20 points. Students will receive:
- 4 points for selecting the correct answer
- 2 points for showing all work and using mathematical strategies
- 1 point for correctly identifying the place value in question 5
- 1 point for neat and organized work

Extension:
For students who finish early, they can create their own multiple choice question about numbers and challenge their classmates to solve it.

Conclusion:
This assignment will assess students' understanding of numbers in a fun and engaging way. Make sure to use your time wisely, show all your work, and pay attention to the place value of numbers. Good luck!
